Privacy Policy

How TREMHUB collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: April 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account details you provide, such as your name, email, profile information, and business listing content. We also collect technical data needed to keep the platform secure and operational.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to run TREMHUB, display member and business profiles, improve features, send important account notices, and maintain community safety.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Profile and listing details you choose to publish may be visible to other users and visitors of the platform.

4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as your account remains active or as needed for legal, security, and operational purposes.

5. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time from your profile settings. Deletion permanently removes your account data from active systems.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your data. No internet-based service can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we continually improve protections.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates become effective when published on this page.

8. Contact

If you have privacy questions, please contact the TREMHUB administrators.
