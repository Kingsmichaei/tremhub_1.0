1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember settings and improve user experience.

2. Cookies We Use

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and maintain secure sessions. We may also use functional cookies to remember preferences.

3. Why We Use Cookies

Cookies help us provide core platform features, improve reliability, and understand general usage patterns to improve TREMHUB.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.
