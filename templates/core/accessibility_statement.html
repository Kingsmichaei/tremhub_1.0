Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to inclusive and accessible digital experiences.

Last updated: April 28, 2026

1. Accessibility Commitment

TREMHUB is committed to making our platform usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

2. Measures We Take

We work to support keyboard navigation, readable color contrast, responsive layouts, semantic structure, and clear text alternatives where appropriate.

3. Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review and improve features, templates, and components to enhance usability across devices.

4. Known Limitations

Some areas may not yet fully meet every accessibility standard. We are actively working to resolve issues as they are identified.

5. Feedback and Support

If you experience accessibility barriers, please contact the TREMHUB administrators with details so we can improve your experience.
